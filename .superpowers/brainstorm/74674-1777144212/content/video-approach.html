LinkedIn Video — How Should the Story Flow?

You and your wife visited a Berlin apartment. Pick the narrative structure that best fits your vision.

A

Problem → Solution (Classic Demo)

Hook: "We're moving to Berlin — how do you pick the right apartment?"
Act 1: Search the address on the map
Act 2: Ask the AI about transport, schools, supermarkets, parks
Act 3: Reveal a connectivity score / summary answer
CTA: "This is what AI + geospatial data can do"

Best for: professional audience, clear product demo

B

Story-First (Emotional Hook)

Hook: "Today my wife and I visited an apartment in Berlin — before signing anything, I pulled out my laptop."
Act 1: Set the scene — apartment address search, map zooms in
Act 2: Live questions to the AI: transport? grocery? parks? hospitals?
Act 3: Decision moment — "We said yes."
CTA: "Built this tool so moving decisions are smarter"

Best for: viral LinkedIn storytelling, personal/human angle

C

Question-by-Question Journey

Hook: "5 questions I asked AI before renting an apartment in Berlin"
Each scene = one question typed into the UI:
1. "What's nearest to [address]?"
2. "How far is the U-Bahn?"
3. "Are there supermarkets within walking distance?"
4. "Find parks or green spaces nearby"
5. "Is this a well-connected neighborhood?"
CTA: "Here's the tool I used"

Best for: listicle format, high shareability, easy to edit
